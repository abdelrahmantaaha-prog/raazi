Where Raazi is

On this computer, the default. From another machine, or over Tailscale, the address shown under LAN mode in Raazi's Settings.

Settings → LAN mode in Raazi shows it. Leave empty for a Raazi on this computer.

A page that reads as a whole article goes straight to the inbox, and the popup closes itself. Anything thinner still waits for the button, in case you want to select the text first. Saved as soon as you tick it.

Sites refuse to be shown inside another page. With this on, that refusal is removed for pages Raazi opens in its inbox's Page view — only there, and only for Raazi at the address above. Send from Raazi while an article is open there and that article is what goes.
